# Only virtue is good; other things are indifferent

The Stoics held that health, wealth, reputation, and pleasure are neither good nor bad for happiness, but 'indifferent'. Only virtue benefits a person, because benefiting is the defining feature of what is good, just as heating defines what is hot. ==The virtuous person therefore remains equally happy under torture or in comfort==, since virtue is the one thing that truly matters. The author notes that this thesis is one of the most contested Stoic doctrines, and that critics argued it leads to a dilemma about why we should pursue health at all.
