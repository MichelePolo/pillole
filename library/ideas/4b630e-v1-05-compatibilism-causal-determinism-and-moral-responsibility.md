# Compatibilism: causal determinism and moral responsibility coexist

The Stoics held that every event is caused by a chain of prior causes, so the present is fully determined by the past. To preserve moral responsibility they distinguished types of causes, arguing that an external push is only an auxiliary cause while the agent's own nature is the decisive one. The author presents this as the Stoic compatibilist strategy, which allowed them to say that actions remain 'up to us' even though they are determined.
