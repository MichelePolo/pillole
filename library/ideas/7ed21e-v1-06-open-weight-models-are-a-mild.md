# Open weight models are a mild risk compared to labs

The author argues that open weight models represent the mildest of the AI risks discussed, because they are released after testing and after comparable models have already been available through APIs. The author also argues that open models would need a training corpus stripped of dangerous scientific domains to pose a danger, and that they are not yet at that level. He adds that limiting widespread access to defensive cybersecurity tools provided by LLMs may itself create the very danger it seeks to prevent, since maintainers lack the tools that malicious actors can access. The author holds these as his own positions, not as established facts.
