# Preferred indifferents still deserve active pursuit

Although health and wealth do not affect happiness, the mainstream Stoic view holds that they are 'preferred' and that choosing them over their opposites is a proper duty in normal circumstances. The virtuous person therefore pursues health, eats well, and exercises, while holding that success or failure does not change her flourishing. The author presents this as the epistemic reading of Stoic indifferents: since we cannot know the future, choosing preferred indifferents is the most responsible way of following the will of Zeus. This reconciles energetic action with inner indifference.
