# Safety of frontier AI needs a joint international body

The author concludes that once LLMs become dangerous enough, the necessary security chain must be built inside labs, and it cannot be established without strong common rules. A single company should not be able to assess independently whether its model is safe enough. According to the author, what is needed is a joint AI safety organization that includes experts from around the world and is recognized by the governments of countries where frontier AI companies operate. The idea is that safety evaluation should not depend on the judgment of one actor alone.
