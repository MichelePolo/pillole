# Stoic philosophy unites logic, physics, and ethics as one system

The Stoics regarded philosophy as three parts: physics, logic, and ethics. They compared it to a living being or an egg, where logic is the bones or outer shell, physics the soul or innermost part, and ethics the flesh or fruit. ==Each part supports the others==, so conclusions in one area tend to reinforce conclusions in another. Mastering all three was seen as required for human flourishing. Modern readers should note that the Stoic 'logic' covered far more than formal argument, including language and epistemology.
