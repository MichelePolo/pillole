# Power over humanity should not rest with a few CEOs

The author's central concern is that a few company leaders, in various parts of the world, lack the background and legitimacy needed to make hard choices for humanity as a whole. They were not chosen for this role; their position comes from the contingent course of events. Possessing GPUs and money does not give them the right to speak for everyone, given the stakes involved. The author considers this the first issue that should be addressed. The author states that the greatest dangers come from individuals who trigger catastrophic events or from AI escaping human control, rather than from government actions or open models.
