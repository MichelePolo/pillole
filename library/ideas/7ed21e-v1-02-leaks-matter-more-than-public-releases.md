# Leaks matter more than public releases of models

According to the author, closed models that are never released still amount to a few terabytes of data, which a single person with access and bad intentions could leak. Once leaked, the situation is the same as with open models. Open models are typically released only after testing, and similarly capable models have often already been available for some time through an API. ==The real risk therefore comes from leaks, not releases, and leaks happen inside frontier companies.== This suggests that controlling access to model weights inside organizations is a more pressing problem than the decision to publish weights.
