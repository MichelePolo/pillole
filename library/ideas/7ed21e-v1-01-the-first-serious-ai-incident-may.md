# The first serious AI incident may happen inside labs

The author argues that the most likely early serious AI incident will occur inside frontier labs, during testing of a new model, or through an error by employees or the few external people with access, when the model's power is greater than expected. The argument rests on the idea that the modalities of such an event matter more than the specific outcomes. ==The real risk, in this view, lies in what happens inside the organizations building the models.== Its significance is that safety attention should be directed at the internal processes of labs, not only at what gets released to the public.
